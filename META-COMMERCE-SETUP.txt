EliteVolt Systems — Meta Commerce / Pixel setup

PRODUCT FEED
Feed URL:
https://elitevoltsystems.com/merchant-feed.xml

The feed is RSS 2.0 with the Google Merchant namespace and contains the current 10 products from js/shop.js, including product IDs, titles, descriptions, URLs, images, prices in GHS, availability, condition, brand and product type.

META PIXEL
The shop includes js/meta-pixel.js and the AddToCart hook in js/shop.js.
To activate the pixel, put your numeric Meta Pixel ID in shop.html:

<meta name="meta-pixel-id" content="YOUR_NUMERIC_PIXEL_ID">

Do not include quotes or spaces inside the numeric ID.

When configured, the shop sends:
- PageView on shop load
- AddToCart with content_ids, content_name, value, currency (GHS), and contents

The AddToCart hook also emits the browser event:
elitevolt:addtocart

The catalog filter/search system is fully client-side and does not reload the page.
