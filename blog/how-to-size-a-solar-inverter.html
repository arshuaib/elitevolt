How to Size a Solar Inverter

Inverter sizing should start with the loads that need to operate simultaneously. You should also account for motor starting currents, PV input limits, battery voltage, phase configuration and whether the system is grid-tied, off-grid or hybrid.

What to consider

  • Estimate your actual daily and peak energy demand.
  • Choose equipment based on compatible electrical ratings and operating conditions.
  • Allow for normal system losses, temperature and installation conditions.
  • Consider future loads and the required backup duration where batteries are involved.
